# Blog Post: Artificial Life In Music

*written by Claus*

[Helen Bledsoe](https://helenbledsoe.com/) is a musician and alife
researcher, who recently wrote a nice text introduction to
[Artificial Life in
Music](https://research.helenbledsoe.com/artificial-life-in-music/) in
their homepage.

The blog post lists several landmark works that mix artificial art and
arts, divided chronologically and by theme. The large number of
citations makes this a must-check reading list for anyone interested
in the theme!

(The entire blog is a great resource, and worth bookmarking and adding
to your feed list).
