# Job openings

## Santa Fe Institute - Postdoctoral Research Fellowship: Network Thermodynamics of Distributed Computation

The Santa Fe Institute — a private, not-for-profit research and
education organization — has an opening for a two-year full-time
postdoctoral fellowship. They are seeking a highly motivated scholar
with expertise in physics (or in special cases in computer science),
who has a desire to apply their expertise to understand the
thermodynamic cost of distributed computation, from digital circuits
and neural networks to human brains.

This position is based in-person in Santa Fe, NM. The desired start
date is no later than June 1, 2027. Interested people should check the
[job posting at the Santa Fe Institute's
webpage](https://santafe.edu/about/jobs/post-doc-thermodynamics)

# NEW CFP and Deadlines

## “Life As It Could Be:  Interdisciplinary Perspectives on Artificial Life” (October 23rd)

** Deadline: October 23rd 2026 **

Date: 7–8 December 2026
Location: Aula Symbiosis, Bioscience Center, Belgium

[This mini-symposium](https://hiw.kuleuven.be/clps/events/symposium-lifeasitcouldbe/home ) will provide a forum for discussion of A-Life’s objectives, overarching theoretical frameworks, main achievements and shortcomings, and prospects for the coming decades of scientific inquiry into what life is and could be—in silicon, in laboratories, and perhaps beyond Earth.

We welcome contributions from theoretical and empirical researchers—philosophers and scientists alike—interested in fostering interdisciplinary dialogues on the past, present and future of A-Life as a scientific field. 

We invite submissions of abstracts (max. 500 words) for presentations. Abstracts must include affiliation and contact information. Please send the abstracts to Alejandro Fábregas-Tejeda via email (alejandro.fabregastejeda@kuleuven.be).
